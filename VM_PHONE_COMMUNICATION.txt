PHONE <-> VM COMMUNICATION BEHAVIOR
==================================

WHAT THIS DESCRIBES
-------------------
This describes only how the phone, its camera, and the VM communicate while
the vehicle counter is running. It does not describe setup, installation, or
the internal technology.

WHO OWNS THE CAMERA
-------------------
The camera belongs to the phone. The VM never opens, controls, or watches the
camera directly. The phone captures what the camera sees and sends still
pictures to the VM for analysis. The VM sends back a description of what it
saw, and the phone draws that description over its own live preview.

WHAT THE PHONE DOES
-------------------
- Keeps one live connection open to the VM for the whole camera session.
- Captures a still picture from its camera and sends it to the VM.
- Waits for the VM's answer before sending the next picture.
- Draws boxes, vehicle labels, and counts over its own preview.
- Lets the user start, stop, reset, change sensitivity, and switch cameras.

WHAT THE VM DOES
----------------
- Waits for the phone to connect.
- Tells the phone when it is getting ready and when it is ready.
- Receives one picture at a time, identifies vehicles, follows each vehicle
  from picture to picture, and keeps the running totals.
- Sends back a short description of that picture: what vehicles are visible,
  where they are, what type they are, and the totals so far.
- Never sends video back. It sends descriptions only.

AT A GLANCE
-----------
Phone: start camera
Phone -> VM: I am starting a camera session
VM -> Phone: getting ready
VM -> Phone: ready
Phone -> VM: picture 1
VM -> Phone: vehicles seen + totals + timing
Phone -> VM: picture 2
VM -> Phone: vehicles seen + totals + timing
...
Phone -> VM: reset
VM -> Phone: totals cleared
Phone -> VM: stop
VM: ends the session

OPENING A SESSION
-----------------
1. The user starts the camera on the phone.
2. The phone opens a secure connection to the VM and keeps it open.
3. The VM answers that it is getting ready, then sends a ready message.
4. Until ready arrives, the phone shows a connecting or warming-up state.
   The phone's own camera preview may already be visible; the boxes and
   counts begin only after the VM is ready.
5. When ready, the phone sends its chosen sensitivity setting to the VM.

SENDING PICTURES
----------------
- The phone sends one still picture at a time.
- It does not send the next picture until the previous one has been answered.
  This prevents a backlog when the VM is slower than the camera.
- The local preview stays smooth because it comes straight from the camera.
  Only the boxes and counts slow down when the VM is busy.
- The phone aims for a steady stream of up to about 25 pictures per second.
  The real rate is whatever the phone-to-VM round trip can support. In this
  VM it is about 9 to 10 pictures per second.
- While the page is hidden or the screen is off, the phone stops sending.
  It resumes when the page is visible again.
- When the user switches cameras, the phone stops using the old camera and
  continues with the new one. The connection stays open and counting
  continues.

WHAT COMES BACK FOR EACH PICTURE
--------------------------------
The VM's answer describes the picture it has just analyzed:
- every vehicle it sees in that picture: its type, its tracking number, its
  confidence, and its position in the picture;
- how many vehicles of each type are visible right now;
- the running total for each type since the session started;
- the overall total for the session;
- how long the VM took to analyze the picture;
- how many pictures per second are currently being answered;
- the size of the picture, so the phone can line up the boxes correctly.

The phone uses this answer to draw the boxes and labels and to update the
counters. The VM never returns an annotated picture or video.

COUNTING BEHAVIOR
-----------------
- A vehicle is added to the session total the first time the VM sees and
  follows it.
- A vehicle that stays in view is not counted again on later pictures.
- "In view now" rises and falls with what is visible in the current picture.
- A vehicle that leaves and later returns may be treated as a new vehicle and
  counted again.
- Totals belong to one camera session. Stopping and restarting the camera
  starts a new session with zero totals.

CONTROLS FROM THE PHONE
-----------------------
- Reset: the phone clears its own display and asks the VM to clear the
  session totals. The VM confirms with zeroed totals. The camera keeps
  running.
- Sensitivity: the phone tells the VM how strict detection should be. The VM
  applies the new setting to later pictures. Totals already counted are kept.
- Switch camera: the phone changes which camera it uses. The connection stays
  open and the totals continue.
- Stop: the phone closes the connection and releases the camera. The VM ends
  the session and frees its capacity for another phone.

READY, BUSY, AND ERRORS
-----------------------
- If the VM is not ready, it tells the phone to wait. The phone shows a
  connecting or warming-up state.
- If the VM is already serving its maximum number of phones, it refuses the
  new session and tells the phone that it is busy. The phone shows the reason.
- If one picture cannot be read or is too large, the VM rejects only that
  picture and keeps the session alive. The phone continues with the next one.
- If the VM cannot start its analysis at all, it sends a fatal error. The
  phone stops the camera and shows that the service is unavailable.

RECONNECTION
------------
- If the connection drops while the camera is running, the phone shows that
  it is reconnecting and tries again automatically, waiting a little longer
  after each attempt.
- When it reconnects, the VM starts a fresh session. The phone's totals begin
  again from zero.
- If the user stopped the camera deliberately, the phone does not reconnect.

MULTIPLE PHONES
---------------
- Each phone has its own connection, its own detections, and its own totals.
- The VM can serve several phones at the same time up to its limit. Each phone
  sees only its own counts.
- When the limit is reached, an additional phone is told that the VM is busy.

WHAT THE USER SEES
------------------
- The phone's own camera preview, always live and local.
- Boxes and vehicle labels drawn over the preview as answers arrive.
- A live, connecting, or off indicator.
- The running total, the count for each vehicle type, and the number in view.
- The analysis delay, the pictures-per-second rate, the picture size, and the
  type of compute in use.
- A message when the security warning must be accepted, when the VM is busy,
  or when the connection is lost.

PRIVACY AND BOUNDARIES
----------------------
- The VM never opens or controls the phone's camera.
- The phone sends still pictures for analysis; it does not send a video feed.
- The VM sends descriptions and counts back; it does not send video.
- The VM does not store the pictures or keep a recording.
- Both sides must be able to reach each other over the network.
- The connection is encrypted. The first time, the phone shows a security
  warning that must be accepted before the camera can start.

END OF SESSION
--------------
- The phone closes the connection when the user stops the camera or leaves the
  page.
- The VM ends the session, frees the capacity, and discards the session totals.
- Starting again creates a new session and a new count.
